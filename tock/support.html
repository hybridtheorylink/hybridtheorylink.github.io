App Overview

Tock is a countdown app that keeps nagging until you answer. Set a length or pick a moment, and when time is up it fires a time-sensitive reminder with Done, Snooze 5 min, and Snooze 10 min — then comes back on a cadence until one of them is tapped. Everything is stored on your device; there is no account and no sync.

Key Features

  • Countdowns two ways: a length (COUNTDOWN) or a date and time (PICK A TIME)
  • Repeat rules for timed countdowns: once, pick days, monthly, yearly
  • Reminders that repeat until answered — global cadence with a per-countdown override, no "gives up on its own"
  • System alarms (iOS 26+): a countdown can ring like the Clock app, through silent mode and Focus
  • Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, per countdown or on by default
  • Quick Start widget with your saved types — buttons start countdowns without opening the app
  • A NOW queue that holds the screen until each elapsed countdown is answered or snoozed
  • A Done tab that archives finished countdowns instead of deleting them, with restore
  • Optional floating countdown bar (Picture in Picture)
  • Works fully offline — your data stays on your device

Requirements

  • iOS 17 or later
  • System alarms require iOS 26 or later
  • Notifications must be allowed for reminders to be delivered; system alarms also need the alarm permission (asked the first time a countdown wants one)

Troubleshooting

  • Reminders aren't arriving: Open Settings › Notifications › Tock and make sure notifications are allowed, and check that a Focus mode or silent mode isn't silencing them. If you need a countdown to ring no matter what, turn on its System alarm switch (iOS 26+) — a system alarm rings through silent mode and Focus.
  • My reminder never fired: iOS keeps only the 64 soonest pending notifications per app, and Tock budgets its 20 system alarms soonest-first, so a long list of far-future countdowns may not all be pre-armed. Each countdown is scheduled as its time approaches; open the app to force a re-sync at any time.
  • The system alarm toggle is greyed out: Alarm permission was denied. Re-enable it in Settings › Tock › Alarms. Until then the countdown automatically falls back to notifications — it is never left silent.
  • Live Activity isn't showing: Check Settings › Tock › Live Activities, and the per-countdown switch in the detail sheet. The "Live Activity by default" setting applies to new countdowns started in the app.
  • I tapped a widget button and nothing happened: The countdown did start — the tile shows the running countdown for about a minute to confirm, and the Live Activity appears without opening the app. A widget-started countdown that wants a system alarm gets one the next time the app becomes active.
  • A repeating countdown disappeared from NOW: That is by design — a repeating countdown rolls to its next occurrence when it fires, so it gets exactly one alert and never queues.
  • I deleted a countdown by mistake: Deleting is immediate and there is no trash — but marking done instead moves it to the Done tab, where it can be restored. Data is local only, so there is no server copy to recover from.
  • Reminders stopped after I restored a backup or reinstalled: Tock stores everything locally; uninstalling removes the data. There is no cloud sync to pull from.

Privacy & Data

Tock stores all your countdowns, types, and settings locally on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics. For full details, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Tip: When you contact support, please include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of what you were doing when the issue happened. That helps us reproduce and fix it faster.
