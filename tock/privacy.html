Tock Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Tock is a countdown and reminder app. We designed Tock to work without an account and to keep your data on your device.

Data We Collect

Tock minimizes data collection. The countdowns and settings you create never leave your device.

  • On-Device Data: Countdowns you create (titles, times, repeat rules, types, icons, sounds), your saved types, and your preferences are saved locally on your device in iOS storage. This data is not transmitted to us.
  • More Apps List: When you open the "More Apps" screen, Tock requests a list of our other apps from our server. The request contains only a page number and our own App Store ID — no identifiers, no personal data. Our server may keep standard request logs (such as IP address) as part of delivering the response.
  • Diagnostics (optional): If you choose to contact support, the email may include your device model, system version, and app version. We only use this to respond to your request.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not collect names, emails (unless you contact support), or phone numbers.
  • We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

  • Notifications: Optional. Used to deliver local reminders when a countdown runs out, including follow-up reminders until you answer. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Alarms (iOS 26+): Optional. A countdown can be handed to the system so it rings like the Clock app's alarm, ringing through silent mode and Focus. Managed in iOS Settings › Tock.
  • Live Activities: Optional. Shows a running countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Controlled by the per-countdown switch, the Settings default, and iOS Settings › Tock › Live Activities.

Widgets & Live Activities

The Quick Start widget and Live Activity read your saved types and running countdowns from shared on-device storage shared between Tock and its widgets. This data stays on your device and is used only to draw the widget and the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island banner.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share your countdowns with anyone. The only network requests Tock makes are the More Apps list described above and the pages of this support site.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your countdowns, types, and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall Tock. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Children's Privacy

Tock is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes, we will update this page with a new "Last updated" date.
