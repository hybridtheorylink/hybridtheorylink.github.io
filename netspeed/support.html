App Overview

NetSpeed gives you a live view of your network — on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, or a floating Picture-in-Picture window — so you always know your download speed, upload speed, and latency without interrupting what you’re doing.

Key Features

  • Real-time download & upload speed monitoring
  • Live Activity on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Picture-in-Picture floating window — visible across all apps
  • One-tap speed test with download, upload, and latency results
  • 60-second speed history graph
  • Wi-Fi and Cellular network detection

Requirements

  • Live Activities require iOS 16.2 or later
  • Dynamic Island requires a supported iPhone model
  • Picture-in-Picture requires iOS 15 or later

Troubleshooting

  • Live Activity not showing: Ensure Live Activities are enabled in iOS Settings and your device is on iOS 16.2+.
  • Dynamic Island not available: Dynamic Island appears only on supported iPhone models.
  • Picture-in-Picture not visible: Confirm PiP is enabled in iOS Settings and your device is on iOS 15+.
  • Speed results look slow: Results depend on your network conditions and server distance.
Note: Whether you’re streaming, gaming, or on a call, NetSpeed helps you spot slow or unstable connections at a glance — so you always know if the problem is your network.
