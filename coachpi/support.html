App Overview

CoachPi helps personal trainers run their business and stay close to their clients. Coaches manage clients, lesson packages, schedules, and attendance; clients log meals, body measurements, and progress photos, book sessions, and chat with their coach.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do a coach and a client link?

Both sign in and choose their role. The coach can invite a client by their CoachPi number, or the two can scan each other's QR code in person. The coach confirms the link.

How many clients can I have?

The Free plan includes 1 client. Pro allows up to 15 clients and Unlimited removes the limit. Open Settings > Plans to subscribe. Clients never need a subscription.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open Settings > Plans > Manage Subscription, or go to your App Store account settings. Changes take effect according to Apple's rules; existing clients and records are kept if you downgrade.

I subscribed but the app still shows Free.

Open Settings > Plans and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of its data. Remember to cancel any active subscription in your App Store account settings.

Can I export my schedule?

Yes. Coaches can export and import sessions as CSV from Settings.
