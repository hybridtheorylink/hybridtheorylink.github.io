CoachPi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

CoachPi (私教派) is an app for personal trainers and their clients. Coaches manage clients, lesson packages, and schedules; clients log meals, body measurements, and progress photos and share them with the coach they are linked to. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Account: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier from that provider and, if you choose to share it, your name. We use it only to create and sign you in to your CoachPi account.
  • Profile: The name, gender, date of birth, height, weight, bio, and avatar you choose to enter.
  • Coaching records (coaches): The client profiles you create (name, phone number, goals, notes), lesson packages and the payment amounts you record, sessions, attendance, and session notes and photos. CoachPi only records the amounts you type in; it does not process payments between coaches and clients.
  • Health and fitness records (clients): Body measurements, meal logs and meal photos, calorie targets, and body progress photos that you add.
  • Messages: Text, images, and voice messages exchanged between a coach and a linked client, and comments on check-ins.
  • Device token: If you allow notifications, an Apple Push Notification token so we can notify you about messages, bookings, and link requests.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. The app reads your subscription status from the App Store on your device to unlock plan limits.

How We Use Data

  • To provide the app's features: syncing your records across devices, scheduling, messaging, and sharing a client's check-ins with the coach they are linked to.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing Between Coach and Client

When a client links with a coach, that coach can see the client's profile, body measurements, meal logs, and progress photos, and the two can message each other. Either side can unlink at any time; after unlinking, the coach can no longer view the client's records.

Service Providers

  • Hosting: Your data is stored on servers we operate with cloud hosting providers, located in the United States.
  • Meal nutrition estimates: When you ask the app to estimate the nutrition of a meal, the meal description and, if attached, the meal photo are sent to Google (Gemini) to produce the estimate. No account identifier or name is included in that request. Google processes the request solely to return the estimate and does not use the content to train its models.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, in-app purchases, and speech recognition (used only when you dictate a chat message; audio may be processed by Apple).
  • Google: Google Sign-In, if you choose it.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take meal and progress photos and to scan QR codes when linking a coach or client.
  • Photos (add only): to save your QR card to your library.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to record voice messages and dictate text in chat.
  • Notifications: optional.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual records in the app at any time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account in the app; deletion cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

CoachPi is not intended for users under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
