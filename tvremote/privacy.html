Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

TvRemote lets you connect to supported devices on your local network. The app does not require an account and does not include analytics or advertising tracking.

Information on your device

Paired device details and app preferences are stored on your device. Pairing credentials are stored in the system Keychain. Remote commands and text entered for your TV are sent to the selected device over your local network.

Apple services and purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases and provides transaction information to the app to verify and restore access. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy. TvRemote may request app artwork from Apple’s iTunes service and, for supported televisions, from the television.

Local network access

TvRemote requests local network permission to discover and connect to your devices. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.
