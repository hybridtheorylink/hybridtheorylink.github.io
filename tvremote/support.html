Connecting

Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same local network, the TV is awake, and Local Network access is enabled for TvRemote in iOS Settings. Device support and features vary by model.

Purchases

Downloading TvRemote and pairing are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks TV control. To restore a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Apple Watch

Watch control uses the connection on your iPhone. Keep TvRemote available on your iPhone and ensure the watch can reach it.
