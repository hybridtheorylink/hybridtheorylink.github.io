FolderPhotos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

FolderPhotos organizes photos and videos in folders on your Mac. Your media, previews, and organization catalog are processed on your device. We do not operate a service that receives your photo library, and the app has no developer-operated accounts, advertising, or third-party analytics.

Media and information stored on your Mac

When you add a folder, the app reads its photos and videos and available metadata, including file names, paths, file sizes, capture dates, dimensions, camera information, and any location coordinates embedded in those files. It creates local thumbnails and previews so you can browse your library.

The local catalog stores folder access bookmarks, favorites, ratings, labels, tags, albums, saved views, custom properties, and people you manually assign. Profile images you create for people are also stored locally. People organization does not require uploading faces or creating an online account.

Apple Maps and location information

FolderPhotos uses Apple's MapKit and geocoding services to show map tiles and resolve place names. When an indexed photo or video contains GPS coordinates, the app may send those coordinates to Apple to look up the corresponding place name, including during indexing. Map views also request map data from Apple. These requests do not upload the original image or video to us.

The app does not request your Mac's current location. These features use location information already embedded in the files you add. Apple's processing is governed by Apple Maps & Privacy and Apple's Privacy Policy. If you do not want embedded coordinates used for place lookup, remove that metadata from a copy of your files before adding the folder. Core browsing and organization work offline; map tiles and place lookup require a network connection.

Purchases

The optional PRO upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks purchase entitlements and saves its upgrade status locally. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple's App Store privacy terms apply to purchase and account processing.

Permissions and file changes

FolderPhotos uses macOS sandbox access to folders you choose and to the Pictures folder. It does not require access to the Apple Photos library, camera, microphone, contacts, or current location.

Organization features such as favorites, ratings, albums, and people normally change only the local catalog. Optional XMP sidecars and date-writing operations can write metadata when you enable or choose them. Moving, renaming, and moving files to Trash are user-initiated actions with confirmation.

Demo content

A new empty library includes clearly identified demonstration photographs copied from the app bundle into writable local storage. They are not photographs collected from users and require no download during first launch. You can delete individual demo photos or remove the demo folder from your library; it will not be automatically restored after removal.

Support messages

If you contact us at hswillg@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and retain them only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send private photos or sensitive information unless it is necessary for the issue you want help with.

Tracking, analytics, and sharing

The app does not include advertising, IDFA access, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. We do not sell your personal information or upload your photo library to a developer-operated server. Apple may process service requests and purchase information as described above.

Retention and deletion

Your catalog and previews remain in local app storage until removed. Removing a folder from the library removes its indexed entries and previews; it does not delete the originals. Moving a file to Trash changes the actual file and is subject to macOS Trash behavior. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily delete all of its Application Support files. Contact support if you need help removing local data, and back up any organization information you want to keep.

Security and your choices

FolderPhotos uses the macOS app sandbox and security-scoped folder bookmarks. You control which folders are added. Protect your Mac account and backups, and keep macOS updated. A folder on a network drive or a cloud-synchronized location remains subject to that provider's own storage and privacy practices.

Children

FolderPhotos is a general photo organization utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends personal information through a support request, contact us to request its removal.
