Get started

  1. Explore the built-in Demo Photos on first launch.
  2. Choose Add Folder, press Shift–Command–O, or drop a folder into the app.
  3. Browse All Photos, Days, Calendar, or the list view. Open a photo or video to view it with its metadata.
  4. Use favorites, star ratings, labels, tags, and albums to organize your media. Albums and saved views keep files in place.

Free version and PRO

The free version includes one personal folder with its subfolders, one album, and one saved view. The built-in demo folder does not use the personal-folder allowance. PRO is an optional one-time purchase that removes those limits; it is not a subscription. The App Store displays the price for your region.

Remove demo photos

Select a demo photo and use Move to Trash to delete its writable copy. Use Remove Demo Photos in the library banner to remove the entire demo folder from the catalog. Deleted or removed demos are not automatically added again on later launches.

A folder is offline or files seem to be missing

Reconnect the drive or network volume. Open Folders & Drives to inspect its state, rescan it, or locate a folder that has moved. Cached thumbnails may remain available while originals are offline, but full-size viewing and playback require the original files.

A video does not play

Container names such as MP4 and MOV do not guarantee that every codec is supported. Confirm that the original is available and readable, and try a standard H.264 MP4 file. Playback and metadata support can depend on the codec, macOS version, and the app version.

Restore PRO

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchase. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If restoration fails, check your connection and try again. For billing or refund questions, use Apple's billing support.

Will organizing photos change the files?

Favorites, ratings, labels, tags, albums, people, and saved views are normally stored in the local catalog. Optional XMP sidecars, writing file or EXIF dates, moving, renaming, and moving to Trash can change files when you enable or choose those actions. Back up important originals and your organization catalog.

Why do map labels need the internet?

Map tiles and place-name lookups use Apple services. Coordinates embedded in indexed files may be used for these lookups. The app does not request your Mac's current location. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Language and requirements

The current app interface is in English. FolderPhotos requires macOS 15 or later. No FolderPhotos account is required.
