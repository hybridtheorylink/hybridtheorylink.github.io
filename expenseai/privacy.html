ExpenseAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

ExpenseAI is an expense logging app. You log an expense by saying it out loud, typing one line, or snapping a receipt. We designed ExpenseAI to work without an account and to keep your data on your device.

Data We Collect

ExpenseAI minimizes data collection. By default, everything you log never leaves your device.

  • On-Device Data: Your expenses (titles, amounts, categories, dates, notes, receipt photos), your custom categories, pay-cycle rule, currency, and other preferences are saved locally on your device in iOS storage. This data is not transmitted to us. There is no account and no cloud sync.
  • Voice Input: When you hold the input bar and speak, your voice is transcribed with on-device speech recognition. Recognition is required to run on the device; your audio is not sent to us.
  • Receipts: Receipt photos are read with on-device text recognition (Vision OCR). The photo itself stays on your device and is stored with the entry as your own record. In the released app, photos are never uploaded.
  • AI Parsing (default, on-device): By default, the text you spoke, typed, or scanned off a receipt is parsed by Apple Intelligence on your device. In this mode, an expense's content never leaves your iPhone.
  • AI Parsing (optional, cloud): You can choose OpenAI or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen/DashScope) in Settings and enter your own API key. Before a provider is enabled, ExpenseAI shows a dedicated permission screen naming the recipient, server, exact data sent, purpose, and data that stays on your iPhone. Only after you tap “Agree & Use” does ExpenseAI send the text you typed, text transcribed from your voice on-device, or text recognized from a receipt on-device to the selected provider to create an expense draft. Receipt photos, original audio, saved entries, totals, and statistics are not sent.
  • API Keys: If you enter a cloud provider API key, it is saved in the iOS Keychain on your device only. It is set to "this device only" — it is not included in device backups and does not migrate to a new phone. It is sent only to the provider it belongs to, as authentication for your own requests.
  • First-Launch Analytics: Once per installation, ExpenseAI sends a first-launch event to our service. It includes your region and language settings, app version and build, device model, iOS version, and Identifier for Vendor (IDFV). We use this information to measure unique installations and understand app and device compatibility. It is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • More Apps List: When you open the "More Apps" screen, ExpenseAI requests a list of our other apps from our server. The request contains only a page number and our own App Store ID — no identifiers, no personal data. Our server may keep standard request logs (such as IP address) as part of delivering the response.
  • Diagnostics (optional): If you choose to contact support, the email may include your device model, system version, and app version. We only use this to respond to your request.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not collect names, emails (unless you contact support), or phone numbers.
  • We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not have access to your expenses, your receipt photos, or your cloud AI usage — those stay on your device or between you and the AI provider you chose.

Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Optional. Used to turn what you say into expense text with on-device recognition. You can decline and use typing or receipts instead.
  • Camera: Optional. Used to snap a receipt. The photo is processed on your device.
  • Photo Library: Optional. Used to pick an existing receipt photo. The photo is processed on your device.

All permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings › ExpenseAI.

Third-Party AI Services

Cloud AI is optional and off by default. Requests travel directly from your device to the provider you select; ExpenseAI's developer does not receive their contents or responses.

OpenAI

Destination: api.openai.com

Data sent and how: Text you type, on-device voice transcription, or on-device receipt OCR text, plus your OpenAI API key as request authentication. Sent only after you select OpenAI and tap “Agree & Use OpenAI.”

Use: Process the submitted text and return an expense draft. See OpenAI's API privacy information.

Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen/DashScope)

Destination: dashscope.aliyuncs.com

Data sent and how: Text you type, on-device voice transcription, or on-device receipt OCR text, plus your Alibaba Cloud API key as request authentication. Sent only after you select Qwen and tap “Agree & Use Qwen.”

Use: Process the submitted text and return an expense draft. See Alibaba Cloud's Privacy Policy.

The selected provider processes these requests under the agreement and privacy terms associated with the API key you supply. These providers state that they apply technical and organizational safeguards to customer data. We require every third party with which ExpenseAI shares user data to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and required by applicable App Store guidelines.

You can stop all future sharing at any time in Settings by choosing Revoke under Cloud AI permission. ExpenseAI immediately returns to Apple Intelligence and requires fresh permission before any cloud provider can be used again. You can also clear an API key in Settings. Requests already processed by a provider are subject to that provider's retention and deletion terms and controls; use the links above or your provider account to manage them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Expense text is shared with OpenAI or Alibaba Cloud only when you explicitly enable that provider as described above. ExpenseAI also sends the first-launch analytics event and More Apps request described above; those requests go to services operated for the developer and are not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your expenses, photos, and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall ExpenseAI. Uninstalling the app removes local data, so we recommend using the CSV export in Settings to move your ledger to a new phone. Cloud AI requests are retained or deleted by the provider under the terms associated with your API account; ExpenseAI's developer does not receive or retain those requests. We retain first-launch analytics and standard server logs only as long as reasonably necessary for aggregate app analytics, security, and service operation. To ask about deletion of developer-held data, contact us using the address below; because ExpenseAI has no account, we may need information such as your IDFV to identify a record.

Children's Privacy

ExpenseAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
