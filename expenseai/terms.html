App Description

ExpenseAI helps you log expenses by speaking a sentence, typing one line, or photographing a receipt. AI reads the amount, merchant, category, and date, and you confirm before anything is saved. Entries are stored locally on your device, and statistics are organized around your pay cycle rather than the calendar month.

  • AI-suggested entries are drafts: nothing is saved until you review and confirm it. Always glance at the amount before saving.

  • If you enable a cloud AI provider with your own API key, submitted text is sent to that provider. If you separately enable cloud receipt images, a compressed copy of each receipt you choose is also sent for visual parsing. Your use of the provider is governed by its terms, and any costs are billed to your own account. Switching the provider back to Apple Intelligence (the default) stops all cloud requests.
