App Description

PastDue helps you keep track of bills, subscriptions, debts, and food expiry dates. The app stores your tasks locally on your device, sends optional local reminders for upcoming due dates, and offers an optional Smart Extract feature that uses on-device intelligence to pre-fill a new task from a photo or pasted text.
