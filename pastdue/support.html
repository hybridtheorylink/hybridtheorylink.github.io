App Overview

PastDue is one simple place for the things that quietly slip past you — rent and utility bills, streaming subscriptions, debts you've lent or owe, and the food sitting in your fridge. Add an item with a due date, get a gentle reminder, and stay on top of it without the spreadsheets.

Key Features

  • Track bills, debts, subscriptions, and food expiry in one place
  • Overdue, due today, and due soon — visible at a glance
  • Local reminders 2 days before, 1 day before, or on the day, at the time you pick
  • Smart Extract: snap a photo or paste text and let on-device intelligence pre-fill the form (iOS 26 and later)
  • Custom categories with your own icons and colors
  • Search and filter by status or category
  • Statistics view for spending and activity patterns
  • 30+ world currencies
  • Works fully offline — your data stays on your device

Requirements

  • iOS 16 or later
  • Smart Extract requires iOS 26 or later and a device that supports Apple Intelligence
  • Notifications must be allowed for reminders to be delivered

Troubleshooting

  • Reminders aren't arriving: Open Settings › Notifications › PastDue and make sure notifications are allowed. Also check that a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. In the app, go to Settings and confirm "Push reminders" is on and your reminder time is set.
  • Wrong reminder time: The reminder time set in the app is used for items without a specific time. Items with a time use that time instead.
  • Smart Extract isn't available: Smart Extract uses Apple's on-device intelligence and requires iOS 26 or later on a supported device. You can still add tasks manually on any supported iOS version.
  • Smart Extract filled the wrong values: Always review the suggested title, amount, and due date before saving. Tap any field to edit it.
  • Camera or photo library not working: Open Settings › PastDue and allow Camera or Photos access.
  • Currency symbol looks wrong: Open the in-app Settings and pick your preferred currency from the list.
  • I deleted an item by mistake: Items deleted from the list cannot be recovered, since data is stored locally. Completed items can be re-opened from the task detail view.
  • I want my sample data back: In a debug build, use "Reset sample data" in Settings. Release builds start empty by design.

Privacy & Data

PastDue stores all your tasks and settings locally on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics. For full details, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Tip: When you contact support, please include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of what you were doing when the issue happened. That helps us reproduce and fix it faster.
