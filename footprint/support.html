Common Issues

Check-in Not Working

  • Ensure Location permission is enabled: iOS Settings → Footprint → Location → "While Using" or "Always".
  • Make sure you have a network connection for place name lookup (offline check-ins save coordinates and sync names later).
  • Restart the app if check-in is unresponsive.

Photos Not Attaching

  • Go to iOS Settings → Footprint → Photos → set to "All Photos" or "Selected Photos".
  • Check available device storage — photos require free space to save.
  • Restart the app and try again.

Map Not Showing My Visits

  • Pull down to refresh the map view.
  • Check that your filter (This Week / This Month / All Time) is set to "All Time" to see all footprints.
  • If visits are missing after reinstalling, restore from a backup or cloud sync (Footprint Pro).

Apple Health Steps Not Appearing

  • Go to iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Footprint → enable "Steps".
  • Alternatively, open the Health app → Browse → Activity → Steps → tap "Share Data" and allow Footprint.
  • Step data for a given day may take a few minutes to sync after check-in.

Cloud Sync Issues (Footprint Pro)

  • Ensure you are signed into the same Apple ID on all devices.
  • Check your internet connection and try pulling to refresh in the timeline.
  • Log out and log back into your Footprint Pro account under Settings → Account.
  • If data appears missing, do not overwrite — contact us first at support@footprintapp.io.

Subscription / Footprint Pro

  • To manage or cancel your subscription: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Footprint Pro.
  • If you were charged but Pro features are not active, restore purchases: App Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • For billing disputes, please contact Apple Support, as all purchases are processed through the App Store.

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Restart your device.
  • If crashes persist, email us with your device model and iOS version so we can investigate.
