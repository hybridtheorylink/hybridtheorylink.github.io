NoteX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

NoteX is a notes client that connects to a NoteX server you configure. We minimize data collection and do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

NoteX stores app preferences such as your server address, memo folder, selected speech-recognition engine, and any speech-service API key you enter. These preferences remain in the app’s local storage unless you remove the app or change them.

Your NoteX Server

The app sends requests to the NoteX server address you configure. Notes, AI prompts and responses, terminal commands and output, and photos or other attachments you choose may be transmitted to and stored by that server. You control the server and are responsible for its security, availability, and privacy practices. We do not receive this content unless you configure a server operated by us or send the content to us for support.

Speech Recognition

  • Apple speech recognition: If you select the system engine and use dictation, audio and speech data are handled by Apple according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy practices.
  • Doubao speech recognition: If you configure and select Doubao, recorded audio is sent directly to the Doubao speech-recognition service for transcription. Your API key is included to authenticate that request.

NoteX accesses the microphone only after you grant permission and start a voice-input action.

Photos, Camera, and Files

With your permission, NoteX can access photos you select or the camera when you choose to add an image. Selected content is sent to your configured NoteX server as an attachment. The app does not access this content for advertising or tracking.

Recommendations and Support

When you open More Apps, NoteX requests a list of apps from our recommendation service and sends the NoteX App Store ID only to exclude NoteX from the results. Standard network information such as an IP address may be processed by the hosting provider as part of delivering the request. If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request.

Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell your personal information. Data sent to services you choose, including your NoteX server, Apple, and Doubao, is governed by those services’ terms and policies. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations.

Children’s Privacy

NoteX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above identifies the latest version.
