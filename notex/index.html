Notex

Local-first Markdown notes, organized your way.

Write fast memos, build structured notes and databases, and see dated work in a clear monthly calendar. Your workspace remains a folder of ordinary Markdown files that other editors, command-line tools, Git, and AI tools can read.

Notes that stay portable

  • Rich Markdown editing with headings, lists, tasks, quotes, code, tables, images, audio, and attachments
  • Nested pages, search, backlinks, favorites, tags, covers, and a recoverable trash
  • Structured databases with table, board, list, and calendar views

Choose how you sync

Keep a workspace on the device, use iCloud Drive across your Apple devices, or connect securely to a NoteX workspace you operate. Notex does not require a developer account to write local notes.

Calendar and dictation

Turn dated notes and database rows into a monthly plan. Dictate with Apple speech recognition, or optionally configure your own Doubao credentials.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Pro plans unlock calendar and sync features.
