Privacy Policy

App: NetSpeed
Effective date: February 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetSpeed ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

NetSpeed is designed to work with minimal data collection. Depending on features you use, we may process:

  • Network performance data: download/upload speed, session totals, and connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Location data: approximate or precise location if you grant permission, used for coverage map and related functionality.
  • Diagnostic data you send us: if you contact support, we may receive information you include in your email.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app features, including real-time speed monitoring and speed history display.
  • Display coverage map points based on your location permissions.
  • Troubleshoot app issues and improve reliability.

3. Location Permissions

NetSpeed may request location access to support map-based coverage features. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings. Some features may be unavailable without location access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising. Network speed test functionality may connect to third-party endpoints to measure performance.

5. Data Retention

In-app monitoring data is used for feature display and may be stored locally on your device for app functionality. We retain support emails only as needed to respond and resolve issues.

6. Your Choices

  • Manage permissions in iOS Settings (Location, Background App Refresh, Live Activities).
  • Stop monitoring at any time within the app.
  • Contact us to request deletion of support communication you previously sent.

7. Children's Privacy

NetSpeed is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us
