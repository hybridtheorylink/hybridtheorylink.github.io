Footprint

Travel diary & check-in

Your personal travel diary, designed to capture every place you visit and every memory you make. Whether you're crossing continents or exploring your own city, Footprint turns your movements into a living travel story.

  • Check in anywhere — add photos, notes, mood, and weather
  • Interactive map of everywhere you've ever been
  • Chronological timeline with step counts via Apple Health
  • Travel stats: cities, countries, photos, steps, memories
  • Pro: unlimited check-ins, cloud sync, PDF reports, offline maps
Available on App Store

NetSpeed

Real-time network monitor

Monitor your network performance in real time. Know exactly how fast your connection is — without leaving whatever you're doing.

  • Live download & upload speed monitoring
  • Live Activity on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Picture-in-Picture floating window across all apps
  • One-tap speed test with 60-second history graph
  • Wi-Fi / Cellular network type detection
Available on App Store

PoseShot

Photography companion

Your personal photography companion, designed to help you take stunning photos with ease. Whether you're a beginner or an enthusiast, PoseShot gives you everything you need.

  • Clean, intuitive camera built for anyone
  • Visual composition guides: rule of thirds, leading lines, symmetry
  • Hundreds of curated poses for portraits, couples, and groups
  • Personal album storage with custom collections
  • Sign in with Apple — privacy-first, no hassle
Available on App Store
