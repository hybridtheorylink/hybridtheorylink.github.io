App Overview

OneBox is a keyboard-first launcher and everyday toolbox for the Mac. Press one hotkey to open a single box, then search apps, do quick math, run web searches, capture and annotate the screen, reach your clipboard history and snippets, pick colors, start a timer, and run your own commands — all without leaving the keyboard.

Getting Started

  • Press ⌥ Space (Option + Space) anywhere to summon OneBox. You can change the hotkey in Settings.
  • Start typing to fuzzy-search installed apps, then press Return to launch.
  • Type a math expression such as 1024*1.5+60 to calculate inline; press Return to copy the result.
  • Use keyword prefixes to search the web, expand snippets, or trigger a micro-app.
  • Press ↑/↓ to move the selection and Esc to dismiss the box.

Key Features

  • Instant fuzzy app launcher, with Pinyin matching for Chinese app names
  • Inline calculator — type an expression, press Return to copy
  • Web-search keywords for your favorite sites and engines
  • Screenshot capture with annotation tools and pin-to-screen
  • Clipboard history you can search and paste back
  • Text snippets for phrases you type all the time
  • Color picker, countdown timer, and text transforms
  • Custom commands and scripts, plus quick system actions
  • Works fully offline — your data stays on your Mac

Requirements

  • macOS 13 or later
  • Accessibility access is needed for the global hotkey and paste-back
  • Screen Recording access is needed for the screenshot tools

Troubleshooting

  • The hotkey doesn't open OneBox: Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and make sure OneBox is enabled. If the shortcut clashes with another app (for example Spotlight also on Option Space), change OneBox's hotkey in Settings.
  • Screenshots capture a blank or black image: Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, enable OneBox, then quit and reopen the app so the new permission takes effect.
  • Clipboard history is empty: History starts recording after the app launches; items copied before that won't appear. Make sure clipboard history is enabled in Settings.
  • An app doesn't show in results: OneBox indexes apps in your Applications folders. If you just installed something, reopen OneBox to refresh the index.
  • Paste-back doesn't type into another app: Paste-back relies on Accessibility access. Re-check the Accessibility toggle above and try again.
  • A custom command isn't running: Confirm the script path and interpreter are correct and that the file is executable. Review the command's output shown in the results area.

Privacy & Data

OneBox stores your settings, snippets, and clipboard history locally on your Mac. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics. For full details, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Tip: When you contact support, please include your macOS version, Mac model, and a short description of what you were doing when the issue happened. That helps us reproduce and fix it faster.
