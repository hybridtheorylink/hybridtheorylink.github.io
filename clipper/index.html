Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your PIN is used to derive your encryption key and is never stored on our servers. If you forget it, your encrypted data cannot be recovered. Please choose a PIN you can reliably remember.

Can Clipper staff read my clipboard?

No. All data is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. We store only AES-256-GCM ciphertext. It is technically impossible for us to read your clipboard content.

How do I sync to a new device?

Sign in with the same account on the new device and enter your PIN. Clipper re-derives your encryption key locally and decrypts your history — no plaintext is ever sent over the network.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and all encrypted data from our servers.
