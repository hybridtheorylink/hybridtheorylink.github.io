Quick Troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest iOS version.
  • Open NetSpeed and allow Location + Live Activities.
  • Enable Background App Refresh for NetSpeed.
  • Restart the app if monitoring data is not updating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Dynamic Island not shown?

Dynamic Island requires a supported iPhone model and iOS 16.2 or later.

Why does speed update slower in background?

iOS may limit update frequency to save battery and system resources.
