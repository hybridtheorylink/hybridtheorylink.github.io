How Can We Help?

Your Notion databases, Google Calendar and iPhone calendars in one calendar.

How It Works

  1. Connect the calendars you use: your Notion account, one or more Google accounts, and/or the calendars already on your iPhone.
  2. In Settings → Connect Calendar, choose which Notion databases and calendars to show, each with its own color.
  3. Nomo shows every page and event in a day, week or month view.
  4. Tap an entry to view or edit it. Drag to reschedule — changes are saved back to Notion or Google Calendar instantly.

Features

  • Notion databases, Google Calendar and iPhone calendars (iCloud, Exchange and more) in one view
  • Day, week and month layouts
  • Tap any day to create a new event or Notion page
  • Edit titles, dates and times directly from the app
  • Drag and drop to reschedule, saved straight back to the source calendar
  • Several Google accounts at once
  • Pick a color for each calendar or database

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • At least one of: a Notion account (free or paid) with a database that has a Date property, a Google account, or calendars on your iPhone

Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't nomo connecting to my Notion account?

Make sure you complete the full OAuth authorization flow in Safari. If the connection fails:

1. Go to Settings → Sign Out of Notion, then try connecting again.
2. Check that you have an active internet connection.
3. In your Notion account under Settings → Connections, remove nomo and re-authorize.

If the issue persists, please email us with your iOS version and a description of where the flow stops.

My Notion database isn't showing up in the database picker.

nomo only displays databases that have at least one Date property. If your database isn't listed:

• Make sure the database has a property of type Date (not a formula or rollup that produces a date).
• Try disconnecting and reconnecting your Notion account to refresh the list.
• Ensure you granted nomo access to the correct Notion workspace during authorization.

How do I add a new entry to my calendar?

Tap any day on the calendar to create a new page in your selected Notion database. A quick-entry form will appear where you can set the title and any other properties. The new page is saved to Notion instantly.

How does drag-and-drop rescheduling work?

Long-press any calendar entry to lift it, then drag it to a new date and release. nomo updates the Date property of the corresponding Notion page immediately. You should see the change reflected in Notion within seconds.

If the sync fails (e.g., due to a lost connection), nomo will show an error. The entry will snap back to its original date — simply try again once your connection is restored.

Can I display more than one Notion database at the same time?

Yes. In the app settings, you can add multiple databases and assign each one a different color. All entries appear together in one unified calendar view, color-coded by their source database.

A change I made in nomo isn't showing in Notion (or vice versa).

Pull down on the calendar to manually refresh. nomo fetches the latest data from Notion on launch and on pull-to-refresh. Changes made in nomo are synced immediately, but Notion's own app may take a moment to reflect them. If sync issues persist, try disconnecting and reconnecting your Notion account.

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Go to Settings → Connect Calendar and tap Sign in with Google. Google's sign-in page opens in the browser; choose your account and allow Nomo to see your calendar list and view and edit your events. Your Google calendars then appear under the Google group, where you can choose which ones to show.

To add more accounts, tap Add Another Google Account in the same place.

Why does Nomo ask for access to my Google Calendar?

Nomo asks for exactly what it needs to work as your calendar:

• See your list of calendars — so you can pick which Google calendars to show. Nomo never changes this list.
• View and edit events — so your events appear in Nomo, and so events you create, edit, drag or delete in Nomo are saved to Google Calendar. Nomo only changes an event when you do.
• Your email address — to tell your Google accounts apart.

Your Google data goes directly between your iPhone and Google. It is never stored on our servers, shared, sold, or used for ads or AI. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I added Google in iOS Settings. Do I also need to sign in with Google in Nomo?

No. Google accounts added in iOS Settings → Calendar → Accounts show up in Nomo through your iPhone's calendars once you allow calendar access. Signing in with Google inside Nomo is an alternative that doesn't depend on iOS Settings. If you use both, the same calendar can appear twice — just show it from one place.

Why can't I drag or edit some events?

Some calendars are read-only — for example holidays, birthdays, subscribed calendars, or calendars shared with you without edit permission. Their events are shown but can't be changed. For repeating events, Nomo asks whether to change just this event or this and all following events when you save or delete; dragging moves only that one occurrence.

How do I disconnect a Google account?

In Settings, find the Google account under Connected Account and tap Disconnect. Nomo revokes its access with Google and deletes the sign-in token from your device. You can also remove Nomo at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Is my calendar data stored on your servers?

No. Nomo fetches your Notion pages and Google Calendar events directly from Notion and Google and displays them on your device; iPhone calendars stay on your iPhone. We do not operate servers that store your calendar content.

Your Notion and Google sign-in tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never sent to us. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I disconnect my Notion account?

Go to Settings → Sign Out of Notion inside the app. This removes the stored OAuth token from your device. To fully revoke access, also visit your Notion account under Settings → Connections and remove nomo from there.

Does nomo work with Notion's free plan?

Yes. nomo works with any Notion account — free or paid — as long as you have at least one database with a Date property.
