ClipCut Support

Last updated: October 2, 2026

ClipCut is a batch video trimming, color grading and export tool for Mac.

Contact

Email hswillg@gmail.com. Include the app version, your Mac model, macOS version, the format of the clips involved, and a short description of the issue.

Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later
  • Video files your Mac can play (MOV, MP4 and other QuickTime-compatible formats)

Quick start

  1. Choose File > Add Clips…, or drag video files into the Clip Queue on the left.
  2. Select a clip and set its in and out points on the trim track (I and O).
  3. In the Grade tab, pick a look or import a .cube LUT, and adjust color. The look applies to every clip in the group.
  4. Open the Export tab, tick the groups to send (or All), choose a name, folder, codec and bitrate.
  5. Press Export. Progress for each clip is shown in the Queue tab.

Troubleshooting

A clip cannot be added or shows an error

ClipCut reads the formats macOS can decode. If a file does not play in QuickTime Player, convert it to a supported format first.

An export failed or stalled

Check that the destination folder still exists and has free space, then use the retry button on that clip's row in the Queue tab. Clips on a network volume or external disk can stall if the volume disconnects.

My LUT does not appear

Use Import in the LUT grid or File > Import LUT… and choose a .cube file. Only 3D LUTs in the .cube format are supported.

Export shows the Pro screen

ClipCut includes 5 free exports. Importing, trimming, grading and preview are always free. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited exports.

A purchase is missing

Choose ClipCut > Restore Purchases and confirm the Mac is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
