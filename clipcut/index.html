ClipCut

Trim, grade and export a whole batch of clips in one pass.

ClipCut is a batch quick-cut tool for Mac. Drop in a pile of camera files, set in and out points, put a look on them, and hand back a folder of graded deliverables. It is not a multi-track editor — it is the tool for turning forty clips into forty finished files.

Grade by group

  • Sort clips into groups; every clip in a group shares one look
  • Built-in looks, plus your own .cube LUTs with adjustable intensity
  • Temperature, tint, exposure, contrast, saturation and more, previewed live
  • Hold a key to compare with the original

Trim fast

  • Set in and out points with I and O, step frame by frame
  • Hover a clip to scrub it; the timeline shows the whole batch at a glance

Export the batch

  • H.264 or HEVC MP4, hardware accelerated, with resolution, frame rate and bitrate limits
  • Numbered file names in queue order; nothing is ever overwritten
  • Queue more batches while one is running; pause, retry or remove individual clips

Requires macOS 14 or later. Editing and preview are free, with 5 free exports; Lifetime Pro unlocks unlimited exports with a one-time purchase.
