ClipCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

ClipCut works entirely on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Your videos and files

The clips you add, the LUT files you import, and the files you export stay on your Mac and on the volumes you choose. ClipCut does not upload them anywhere, and it makes no network connections of its own. It reads only the files and folders you select, and writes only to the destination folder you choose (the Movies folder by default).

Information stored on your device

ClipCut stores your preferences on your Mac: export settings, window layout, imported LUTs, and the number of free exports used. This information is not sent to us. Removing the app and its data removes it, subject to normal operating-system and backup behavior.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is purchased through the App Store. Payment and purchase records are handled by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy practices. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it only to respond to you.

Children

ClipCut is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. Questions or privacy requests: hswillg@gmail.com.
