Common Issues

Camera or Photo Library Access

  • Open iOS Settings → PoseShot → allow Camera and Photos permissions.
  • Restart the app after changing permissions.

Saving Photos Fails

  • Ensure Photos permission is set to “All Photos”.
  • Check device storage and try again.

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Update to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Restart your device.
