FilePort Support

FilePort connects your iPhone, iPad and Mac to SMB, SFTP and WebDAV servers. Questions or problems? Email hswillg@gmail.com and include your device, OS version and the kind of server you're connecting to.

Common Questions

My NAS doesn't appear under “Found on Your Local Network”.

Make sure FilePort is allowed to use the local network (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). You can always type the server's address, such as 192.168.1.20 or nas.local.

Which address should I use for an SMB share?

Enter the server's name or IP address. Leave Share empty to see every share on the server, or enter one share to go straight to it.

Can I sign in to SFTP with a key?

Yes. Choose Private Key, then import an OpenSSH Ed25519 or RSA key. The key is stored in your device's keychain.

Do transfers continue in the background?

Transfers keep running for a while after you leave the app and resume automatically when you return. On iOS 26 and later, long transfers can finish in the background with progress shown by the system.

How do I restore FilePort Pro?

Open Settings › Unlock FilePort Pro and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.
