FileDuo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

FileDuo is a file manager for iPhone, iPad and Mac that connects to file servers you choose — SMB shares on a NAS or computer, SFTP servers, WebDAV services and FTP servers — and that can send files to other devices running FileDuo nearby. This policy explains what FileDuo does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your device except the connections you make to your own servers.

The Short Version

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your files travel only between your device, the servers you add, and the nearby devices you pair with.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Server details: the names, addresses, ports, folders and user names of the servers you add are saved on your device.
  • Passwords and keys: passwords, SSH private keys and passphrases are stored in the system keychain on your device. If you turn off “Remember Password”, the password is kept in memory only until FileDuo quits.
  • SSH host keys: the identity of each SFTP server you connect to is remembered so FileDuo can warn you if it changes.
  • Downloads, previews and thumbnails: files you download are saved to FileDuo's Downloads folder (or a folder you choose); files you open for preview and small thumbnails are kept in FileDuo's storage on your device. You can remove them at any time in Settings › Storage.
  • Nearby devices and messages: the names and security identities of devices you have paired with, and the messages and files exchanged in Nearby chats, are stored on your device. You can clear a chat, or forget a paired device in Settings › Nearby, at any time.
  • Recents, favorites, transfer history and a search index of folder names and file names you have browsed, so search and Recents work instantly.

None of this information is sent to us or to any third party.

Network Connections

FileDuo connects directly to the servers you add, using SMB, SFTP (SSH), WebDAV (HTTP/HTTPS) or FTP. On the local network, FileDuo uses Bonjour to show servers that advertise themselves and to find other devices running FileDuo, which is why it asks for Local Network access. FileDuo also loads a list of our other apps when you open More Apps in Settings; that request contains no personal information.

Nearby Devices

When you send files or messages to another iPhone, iPad or Mac running FileDuo, the two devices connect to each other directly over your local network (or a direct peer-to-peer Wi-Fi link). Each device is paired once by confirming the same six-digit code on both screens, and everything that passes between them is encrypted. Nothing is relayed through our servers or anyone else's. Folders you choose to share can be browsed only by devices you have paired with.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FileDuo shows a local notification when a Nearby message or file arrives. Notifications are created on your device; no push service or notification server is used.

Purchases

FileDuo Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Photos and Files You Upload

When you upload from Photos or the Files app, FileDuo reads only the items you pick and sends them to the server you chose. FileDuo does not access the rest of your library.

Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message (the app pre-fills your app version, OS version and language). We use it only to reply.

Children

FileDuo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
