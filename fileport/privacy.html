FilePort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

FilePort is a file manager for iPhone, iPad and Mac that connects to file servers you choose — SMB shares on a NAS or computer, SFTP servers, and WebDAV services. This policy explains what FilePort does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your device except the connections you make to your own servers.

The Short Version

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your files travel only between your device and the servers you add.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Server details: the names, addresses, ports, folders and user names of the servers you add are saved on your device.
  • Passwords and keys: passwords, SSH private keys and passphrases are stored in the system keychain on your device. If you turn off “Remember Password”, the password is kept in memory only until FilePort quits.
  • SSH host keys: the identity of each SFTP server you connect to is remembered so FilePort can warn you if it changes.
  • Offline files, previews and thumbnails: copies of files you download for offline use, files you open for preview, and small thumbnails are stored in FilePort's storage on your device. You can remove them at any time in Settings › Storage.
  • Recents, favorites, transfer history and a search index of folder names and file names you have browsed, so search and Recents work instantly.

None of this information is sent to us or to any third party.

Network Connections

FilePort connects directly to the servers you add, using SMB, SFTP (SSH) or WebDAV (HTTP/HTTPS). On the local network, FilePort uses Bonjour to show servers that advertise themselves, which is why it asks for Local Network access. FilePort also loads a list of our other apps when you open More Apps in Settings; that request contains no personal information.

Purchases

FilePort Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Photos and Files You Upload

When you upload from Photos or the Files app, FilePort reads only the items you pick and sends them to the server you chose. FilePort does not access the rest of your library.

Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message (the app pre-fills your app version, OS version and language). We use it only to reply.

Children

FilePort does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
