App Description

RemoteTimer lets you create and run named timers on your iPhone and iPad, view live running and waiting time, and control a timer remotely from a paired Apple Watch or another nearby device. Remote commands (Start, Resume, End) are sent directly between your devices over the local network and WatchConnectivity. The app also offers Picture-in-Picture to keep a timer visible while you use other apps. All timer data is stored locally on your device.
